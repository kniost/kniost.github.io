Nature Day: order a limited-time globe between April 15 and 22

The cool globe is only orderable these 8 days each year; get the hedge DIY recipe from Leif

Nature Day is about as simple as events get: every year from April 15 to 22, in both hemispheres, Nook Shopping offers a limited-time cool globe for 2,300 Bells. Order one on any of those 8 days and you're done — no prep needed.

The other Nature Day–adjacent item is the hedge DIY recipe, which comes from Leif, the gardening sloth: buy something from him on 3 different days, any time of year. Older guides online mention "earning 5x Nook Miles for eco-friendly tasks" — that was how the event worked in the game's first year, and it's gone now. There's a quick rundown at the end.

01When it happens

Nature Day is fixed at April 15 through April 22 every year, at the same time in both hemispheres. Unlike Bunny Day, the dates never move.

Nobody comes to host, and nothing gets set up in the plaza, so it's very easy for the week to slip by unnoticed. Luckily, you'll get a "Limited-Time Offer from Nook Shopping!" announcement in-game reminding you that the globe is available.

02How to order the globe

Open the Nook Shopping app on your NookPhone, or use the Nook Stop in Resident Services, and look in the limited-time section:

ItemPriceAvailable
cool globe2,300 BellsApril 15–22

It arrives in your mailbox the next day. Once April 22 has passed, you can't order it from the catalog either — you'll have to wait another year. So make time to place an order during the week.

The cool globe is a small item you can put on the floor or on top of tables and dressers. It's a different item from the regular globe Nook's Cranny sells, so don't mix them up.

03Where to get the hedge DIY recipe

The hedge is the green shrub fence you see lining mazes. It takes 10 clumps of weeds, 5 tree branches, and 2 stones, all of which you can pick up around your island. There's only one way to get the recipe: buy something from Leif on 3 different days, and he'll give it to you. No matter how many things you buy in one day, it only counts once, so just buy one item per visit and spread it out over three days.

TipIf you're worried about forgetting the dates, check the Event Calendar in Nook Circle. It lists the Northern and Southern Hemispheres separately and includes Nook Shopping limited-time offers like Nature Day.

04A few more things

If all you want is the globe, you can order it any day from April 15 to 22 — no need to be there on day one. If you've time traveled, what counts is your in-game date, not the date on your Switch.

The globe only sells for 575 Bells, a quarter of what you paid. One for display is plenty; ordering extras isn't worth it.

Right after Nature Day comes May Day on April 29. Tom Nook will give you a ticket to a maze island, so set aside some time to go.

05Version history

In the game's first year (2020), Nature Day was a Nook Miles+ event from April 23 to May 4. Each day you'd get an extra task with a green border and a sprout icon, mostly about plants and trees (transplanting trees, planting flower seeds, giving flowers to villagers, crafting flower crowns, and so on), and completing one earned 5 times the usual miles. After finishing your first one, Tom Nook would give you the hedge DIY recipe.

The 1.9.0 update in 2021 retired that event and replaced it with the current Nook Shopping offer. That's also when the cool globe was introduced and the hedge recipe moved to Leif. The 5x miles bonus is gone for good — it won't come back even if you set your island's clock back to 2020. Nothing changed in 2.0 or 3.0.
