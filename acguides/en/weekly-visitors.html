Weekly visitors: who comes when, and what they do

Daisy Mae, K.K., Redd, Saharah, and more in one table — plus Harv's Island and Kapp'n

Besides your residents, your island gets a steady stream of visitors: Daisy Mae selling turnips on Sunday, K.K. performing in the plaza on Saturday night, and a weekday rotation of Redd, Saharah, Leif, C.J., and others. Most of them only stay for a day, and if you miss them you'll have to wait for the next round. So figuring out who's around when you log in each morning makes planning a lot easier.

The weekend is pretty much set in stone: Daisy Mae on Sunday, K.K. on Saturday. Monday through Friday, at most one visitor comes per day, on a rotation. Celeste and Wisp only show up at night and don't take up that slot, so they can appear on the same day as a daytime visitor. Harv's Island also has a market, so on days when Leif, Kicks, Saharah, or Redd aren't on your island, you can find them there.

01How the schedule works

The day changes over on your island at 5 AM. Apart from Celeste and Wisp, your island gets at most one special visitor per day. Who comes that week, and on which days, is decided at the start of the week. Not everyone gets a slot every week; anyone who misses out gets priority the following week. So if a visitor hasn't shown up in a while, don't worry — they'll most likely come next week.

The morning announcement will mention who's visiting that day. Listen to it after you log in and you'll know where to look. Some visitors set up shop in the plaza in front of Resident Services, where you can't miss them; others wander around the island, so you'll have to check along the beaches and rivers yourself.

03Making the most of each visitor

Daisy Mae and K.K.

Daisy Mae only starts visiting once Nook's Cranny is built, and Sunday morning is the only time you can buy turnips from her — she leaves at noon. If you're playing the stalk market, make finding her the first thing you do on Sunday.

K.K.'s regular performances start once your island hits a 3-star rating and he's played his first concert. Every Saturday from 6 PM to midnight you can request a song, and he'll give you a copy afterward. If there's an event like a Fishing Tourney or Bug-Off in the plaza on Saturday, K.K. comes on Sunday instead; if Sunday's booked too, he moves to Monday. He'll also make a special visit on your birthday.

C.J. and Flick

These two only start visiting after Resident Services has been upgraded to its full building. They buy fish and bugs at 1.5 times the Nook's Cranny price — 50% more than usual. Hold off on selling the fish and bugs you've stocked up around the Fishing Tourney and Bug-Off; save them for when these two come around.

Give them 3 of the same fish or 3 of the same bug, and they'll send you a model of it the next day. If you want models, gather the sets while they're on your island. C.J. won't take your fish until you've completed his fishing challenge.

Redd

Redd starts visiting once you've donated 60 items to the museum. On his first visit, he isn't on his boat — he wanders the island and tries to sell you a painting for 4,980 Bells. This one is always genuine, so buy it and donate it to Blathers to unlock the museum's art gallery expansion.

From his second visit on, his boat docks at the small beach on the north side of your island, and he offers 4 pieces of art at 4,980 Bells each — some real, some fake. The museum won't accept fakes.

Saharah

Saharah doesn't set up a stall; she walks around the island, so you'll need to find her. Her rugs come in small, medium, and large for 1,000, 1,500, and 2,000 Bells, and each comes with 1, 2, or 3 Saharah Tickets respectively. Save up 5 tickets to trade for a piece of mysterious wallpaper or mysterious flooring you don't have yet. If you'd rather not save up, you can also buy mysterious wallpaper or flooring outright for 3,000 Bells each.

Label

Label gives you a fashion theme. Go change into an outfit that fits it, then come back and talk to her. Nail it and she'll reward you with a piece from her own brand, which Able Sisters starts selling the next day. She'll also mail you 1–2 tailors tickets later, each good for one item worth up to 3,000 Bells at the shop.

Gulliver and Gullivarrr

Gulliver washes up asleep on the beach. Wake him up and he'll ask you to find the 5 pieces of his communicator that got scattered. They're buried in the sand — dig them up and bring them to him. The next day he'll mail you a souvenir from abroad. Help him 30 times and he'll send you the golden shovel DIY recipe. If you dig up a part and don't give it back, it'll turn into a rusted part the next day.

Gullivarrr only shows up after you've bought a wet suit. He's dropped his communicator in the ocean, so you'll have to dive in and fish it out. The next day he'll mail you a piece of pirate-themed furniture or clothing.

Gulliver's communicator parts are buried on the beach (Image: Nookipedia)

Celeste and Wisp

Celeste visits at most once a week, and there's a meteor shower on the night she comes. She won't visit on a night when K.K. is performing. Talk to her and each player can learn one DIY recipe per day. Once you've learned all the star- and zodiac-themed recipes, she'll give you star fragments instead.

Wisp only comes out after 8 PM. When you walk up and talk to him, you'll startle him, and his 5 spirit pieces will scatter across the island. Catch them one by one with your net and bring them back, and you can choose between something new or something pricey as a thank-you. Any spirit pieces you don't return disappear the next day.

TipIn the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, you can shout at Gulliver, Gullivarrr, or Wisp with the megaphone to wake them up or scare the spirits loose instantly — no need to stand next to them mashing A.

04The Harv's Island market and Kapp'n's boat tours

The Harv's Island market

Once your island has a 3-star rating and K.K. has played his first concert, a market area opens up on Harv's Island with stalls for Leif, Kicks, Saharah, and Redd. Each stall needs 100,000 Bells before it opens, and you can only open one per day, so it'll take a few days to open them all.

The stalls restock every week, so if you missed something, check back next week. On the day one of them visits your island, their stall on Harv's Island is closed. Even after their stalls open, Leif, Saharah, and Redd still visit your island about once every two weeks, so you can catch them in both places.

Kapp'n's boat tours

Once your island is at 3 stars and K.K. has played his first concert, Kapp'n at the pier can take you out to sea: once a day, for 1,000 Nook Miles, he'll ferry you to a random island. After you've decorated your hotel's first VIP room, you get one extra trip a day for 100 hotel tickets.

The Harv's Island market, where Leif, Redd, and Kicks each have their own stall (Image: Nookipedia)

With this many visitors, it's easy to lose track of who's come by this week and who hasn't. Nook Circle's Island tab has a Visitors section where you can tap each day of the week as visitors arrive, so you can see at a glance who's been.
