Mushroom season: all of November, new mushrooms by the trees every day

Where to find all five mushrooms, digging up rare mushrooms, and the 12 mushroom furniture recipes plus mushroom cooking

In November in the Northern Hemisphere and May in the Southern Hemisphere, mushrooms pop up next to the trees on your island every day. There are five kinds in all: round mushrooms, skinny mushrooms, flat mushrooms, elegant mushrooms, and rare mushrooms.

The first three are common and go into furniture like the mush lamp, mush parasol, and mush partition. Elegant and rare mushrooms are uncommon and sell for 10,000 and 16,000 Bells each, making them the most valuable of all the seasonal materials. There are 12 DIY recipes this season.

All dates are in-game dates. The Southern Hemisphere runs half a year behind the north.

November is a busy month in the Northern Hemisphere: acorns and pine cones are still dropping, and maple leaves arrive from November 16–25. Turkey Day falls on the fourth Thursday of November, and in the Northern Hemisphere Franklin may ask for a mushroom for his gratin, so keep one on hand around then.

02How to find all five

Mushrooms grow next to trees, both hardwood and cedar. 5 new ones appear each day, and they stop growing once there are 30 on your island, so it's best to walk past your trees every day or two and pick them up before they max out.

Rare mushrooms don't grow above ground. They're buried in the dirt next to trees, marked by a crack in the ground, and you have to dig them up with a shovel.

The higher your island rating, the better the mushrooms

The chance of elegant and rare mushrooms depends on your island rating at Resident Services:

Island ratingElegant mushroomRare mushroom
3 stars4%1%
4 stars10%5%
5 stars15%10%

On a 5-star island, roughly 1 in every 4 new mushrooms is a good one.

Mushrooms only grow on your own island, not on the mystery islands you fly to with a Nook Miles Ticket. Kapp'n's mushroom island does have them, though; see section 03.

NoteYou can eat mushrooms too. Just like fruit, they give you a burst of strength, enough to dig up and move trees or break rocks. Check what you're eating first so you don't gobble up an elegant or rare mushroom.

03Where the DIY recipes come from

The big one is shooting down balloons. During mushroom season, balloons floating overhead drop this season's DIY recipes, and they'll keep doing so until you've learned them all.

Next is visiting Resident Services on the first day of the season. On November 1 in the north or May 1 in the south, Tom Nook or Isabelle will give you the mushroom wreath. You only get it on the first day.

The third is Kapp'n's boat tours, covered below.

Missed it? Catch up on Kapp'n's boat

Kapp'n at the pier will take you out to sea once a day for 1,000 Nook Miles. Every so often, he'll drop you on an island stuck in a particular season, and the mushroom island is one of them; each seasonal island has about a 3% chance. On that island you can pick up mushrooms next to the trees and shake down acorns and pine cones, and message bottles on the beach may hold mushroom DIY recipes.

The odds are low, so this is really just luck. There's also a catch: your own island has to have gone through this season at least once before you can be taken to the matching island. Once you've decorated your hotel's first VIP room, Kapp'n will take you on an extra trip each day (for 100 hotel tickets), which gives you a slightly better shot.

Crafting all 12 takes 15 skinny mushrooms, 14 flat mushrooms, 8 round mushrooms, 3 elegant mushrooms, and 1 rare mushroom, plus 16 wood, 10 weeds, 10 tree branches, 5 clay, 3 star fragments, and a log stool.

Elegant mushrooms are only used in the mush wall and forest wall, and rare mushrooms only in the forest flooring. Keep enough of those for these recipes, and selling any extras is no loss.

05What to make first

Mush parasol: a giant mushroom used as a parasol. It really stands out outdoors and takes just 3 flat mushrooms.

Mush lamp, mush low stool, mush table, mush partition: a full set of mushroom furniture. They all need very few materials and can be customized in different colors once you've made them.

Forest wall + forest flooring: turn your room into a mushroom forest. They need elegant and rare mushrooms, so they're the hardest to put together, but also the most impressive.

Mushroom wreath: the one you get on the first day. Hang it on your wall for a cozy fall look.
