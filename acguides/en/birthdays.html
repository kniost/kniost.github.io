Birthdays: yours and your villagers'

What happens at your party, what you get, and the best gifts for a villager's birthday

There are two kinds of birthdays on your island. One is yours: it comes once a year, someone throws you a party the moment you step outside, and K.K. even makes a special trip to perform. The other is your villagers': the birthday villager stays home all day waiting for you to stop by, and the right gift boosts your friendship faster than usual.

Both kinds go up on the bulletin board 8 days in advance. Your own birthday takes no prep — just log in that day and walk out your front door. For a villager's birthday, it's best to pick up a gift and some wrapping paper at Nook's Cranny beforehand and bring them over on the day. Below, we'll start with your birthday, then cover your villagers'.

01Your birthday: a party as soon as you step outside

Your birthday is the date you entered when you created your character. If you picked February 29, you'll celebrate on February 28 in non-leap years.

Eight days ahead, a notice goes up on the bulletin board outside Resident Services, and on the morning of, Isabelle or Tom Nook will mention it again in the announcements.

As soon as you leave your house that day, your best friend among your villagers will run up and whisk you off to their home. The place is all decorated with a cake in the middle and two more villagers waiting. Everyone holds up party poppers and sings your island tune, you blow out the candles, the poppers go off, and you get a birthday present.

Next, they'll hand you a stick and have you whack the piñata hanging in the room. The faster you hit it, the more birthday cupcakes fall out, and in the end your host hands you all of them. After that, the villagers keep dancing and singing in the house, and the party only wraps up once you leave.

When you head out after the party, you'll get a letter from Mom with Mom's homemade cake inside. You'll also unlock a Nook Miles achievement.

After the candles comes the piñata — the faster you hit it, the more cupcakes drop (Image: Nookipedia)

TipYour party is hosted by the villager you're closest to. To find out who that is, once Katrina's stall opens on Harv's Island, you can pay her 1,000 Bells to read your friendship with a particular villager.

02What to do with birthday cupcakes

The birthday cupcakes from the piñata are edible and give you the strength to dig up trees and break rocks. You can also display them as decorations, but you can't sell them.

The better use is to give them to villagers — villagers on other islands count too. If your friendship is high enough, they'll give you something from the birthday series in return:

Birthday seriesType
birthday cake, birthday candles, birthday sign, birthday tableFurniture
birthday hat, birthday shadesClothing
party wall, party flooringWallpaper and flooring

If your friendship isn't high enough yet, or you already have the full set, the villager will just give you an ordinary gift back. So save your cupcakes for villagers you're close to, while you're still missing pieces of the set.

03K.K. sings you a birthday song

On your birthday, K.K. comes to the plaza in front of Resident Services no matter what day of the week it is. He plays "K.K. Birthday," and the credits that roll at the end are replaced with birthday wishes from your villagers. Afterward, he gives you a copy of the song.

Friends who visit your island that day can leave you birthday messages. The first time K.K. plays the song, he'll show you those messages, and afterward they'll arrive in your mailbox as letters from your friends.

If your birthday falls on a Fishing Tourney or Bug-Off day, K.K. comes the next day instead.

Heads upOnce you have "K.K. Birthday," you can't order it from Nook Shopping, so don't sell it on a whim.

The Roost will help you celebrate too: stop by for a cup of coffee on your birthday, and afterward Brewster will wish you a happy birthday.

04Villager birthdays: bring a gift to their house

Villager birthdays also go up on the bulletin board 8 days ahead, with an announcement on the morning of. The birthday villager stays home all day with "K.K. Birthday" playing. Visiting is up to you, but bringing a gift raises your friendship, and they'll mail you a thank-you gift the next day.

What you give makes a big difference to how much your friendship goes up:

GiftFriendshipWrapped
Bugs, fish, or fossils worth 2,000 Bells or more; anything else worth 500 Bells or more+5+8
Bugs, fish, or fossils worth 501–1,999; anything else worth 126–499+4+6
Sea creatures; anything cheaper+3+4
Trash like empty cans, boots, old tires, and weeds−5—

As the table shows, wrapping is well worth it: the same gift, wrapped, jumps from +5 to +8 at the top tier. The easiest move is to buy a piece of furniture or clothing worth 500 Bells or more at Nook's Cranny and wrap it before giving it. Nook's Cranny sells wrapping paper too, so grab some while you're there. Whatever you do, don't pad your gift with trash — that costs you 5 points.

If two villagers share a birthday, they'll celebrate together at one of their houses, and you can give gifts to both. Friends can also visit your island to give the birthday villager a present.

Two villagers who share a birthday party together at one house, both in birthday hats (Image: Nookipedia)

05How to know whose birthday is when

In the game, the bulletin board is your only source, and it only shows birthdays 8 days ahead — not a lot of time to pick out a gift. In Nook Circle, the Villagers section of the Handbook lists every villager's birthday so you can look them up early, and the Home card shows when one of your island's villagers has a birthday that day, so you'll know as soon as you open the app.

Nook Circle's Home screen: when a villager on your island has a birthday, the card shows "[name]'s birthday" that day
