Villagers moving in and out

Keep the ones you love, let go of the ones you don't, and invite the ones you want

You get most of the say in who lives on your island. A villager only moves out in one of two ways: they come to ask and you say yes, or you make room for them when inviting someone new. If you don't agree, nobody packs up and leaves behind your back.

So there are really just three things to do: talk your favorites out of leaving when they get the urge, say yes right away when someone you don't want asks, and quickly invite who you want onto any empty plot. We'll go through them in that order, then finish with how to use amiibo cards to swap villagers by name.

01When villagers want to move

Your island can hold up to 10 villagers, and no one will ask to leave until you have at least 6. With fewer than that, you don't need to worry about it.

Once you have 6 or more, a few timing rules apply:

· After someone moves out, no one else will ask for 15 days.

· After someone asks and you talk them out of it, no one else will ask for 5 days.

· After that, each day nobody asks, the chance that someone asks the next day goes up a little; the more villagers you have, the more likely someone will ask.

A few villagers are temporarily exempt: the most recent move-in, whoever asked last time, anyone with a birthday within 7 days, and anyone whose house is being relocated.

Exactly who gets picked involves some randomness. It's generally believed that the villager you're least close to is more likely to be chosen, so chatting with your favorites often and giving them gifts may lower their odds a bit — but there are no guarantees.

02What to do when you see a thought bubble

A villager thinking about moving wanders the island lost in thought, with a thought bubble over their head. Go talk to them, and they'll ask whether they should move away.

A villager with a white thought bubble over their head — go chat when you see one (Image: Nookipedia)

To keep them, pick the answer that asks them to stay, and they'll drop the idea and stick around.

To let them go, say yes. The next day, visit their house and you'll find them packing, with boxes stacked everywhere; the day after that they're gone, and their plot becomes an empty lot. They're still on your island on packing day, so that's your chance for a last chat.

NoteAvoiding them won't make them move, but the bubble may pass to another villager — and next time it could land on the one you'd miss most. So whenever you log in and see a bubble, go talk: keep the ones you want, let go of the ones you don't.

03Choosing who leaves: amiibo cards

Waiting for the bubble to land on a particular villager is pure luck. If you want to choose who leaves, amiibo cards are the most direct way — and you get to invite the villager you want while you're at it. Here's how:

1. Scan a villager amiibo card at the Nook Stop in Resident Services, and that villager comes to your campsite.

2. Scan them on three different days, crafting one piece of furniture they ask for each time.

3. On the third visit, you can invite them to move in. If your island already has 10 villagers, you get to choose who moves out; the villager you pick packs up that day and leaves the next.

It takes three days, but every step is in your hands — no luck involved. When your island is full, a regular campsite visitor decides whom to replace, while with amiibo cards you decide. That's the biggest difference between the two.

Open a villager in Nook Circle to see their amiibo card series and number

TipIf you have to part with a villager you love, you can pass them on to a friend. On their packing day (when their house is full of boxes), have a friend with an empty plot visit and talk to them a few times, and your friend can take them in.

04Where to find new villagers

Mystery islands

Spend 2,000 Nook Miles on a Nook Miles Ticket and fly to a random deserted island, where you'll sometimes find a villager wandering around. To invite them back, two conditions apply: you need an empty plot that hasn't already been claimed, and you can only invite one villager from mystery islands per day.

Meet a villager on a mystery island, and if your invitation works, they'll move to your island (Image: Nookipedia)

The villager you meet is chosen by first rolling a random species, then a random villager of that species — so the more villagers a species has, the harder it is to find any particular one. To get a sense of how many tickets you might need, check Mystery Island odds on a villager's detail page in Nook Circle.

Campsite

Once your campsite is built, villagers will come camping from time to time. Do what they ask (usually playing a little game with them), and you can then invite them to move in. If your island is full, they'll tell you who they plan to replace first, and you can decline if you don't like the answer.

When your island is full, the camper tells you whom they'd replace first (Image: Nookipedia)

amiibo cards

See the previous section for how. It's the most reliable way to get a specific villager, and the only way to get the Sanrio collaboration villagers.

Villagers packing up on a friend's island

This is the previous tip in reverse: if someone on a friend's island is packing and you have an empty plot, fly over and talk to them a few times to bring them home.

05Don't leave empty plots sitting

When a villager moves out, their plot becomes empty. If you don't invite anyone, the game assigns a random villager, the sign on the plot changes to "Sold," and you can no longer invite anyone else. A random villager might not be to your taste, and replacing them means waiting for their own thought bubble or using an amiibo card to free up the spot — a long way around.

If you want to pick your neighbors, act the same day the plot opens up: fly to mystery islands to try your luck, or check the campsite daily for visitors. If you have the amiibo card you want, it's even easier — no need to wait for an empty plot at all. Invite them while you're full and choose who leaves.

06Can hotel guests move in?

Kapp'n's resort hotel gets short-stay guests every day, including animals who could normally be villagers. But there's no way to invite a hotel guest to move straight to your island; if you want that villager, you'll still need to use one of the routes above.

07Version changes

Ver. 3.0 didn't change any move-out rules, so everything above still applies. Ver. 3.0 added 2 brand-new villagers and brought back 2 from earlier games, bringing the total to 417. The hotel was also added in 3.0.
