Fireworks shows: every Sunday night in August

Isabelle hands out light-up boppers, Redd runs a raffle, and your own designs can light up the sky

Every Sunday night in August, your island hosts a fireworks show. You can get a light-up bopper from Isabelle, try your luck at Redd's raffle, and even submit your own custom designs to be launched as fireworks across the night sky.

There's not much to prep: keep one inventory slot open for Isabelle's bopper, and bring some Bells, since Redd's raffle costs 500 a pull. If you want to see your own designs as fireworks, draw them during the day and hand them in before the show starts.

01When it happens

Fireworks shows take place every Sunday in August, on the same days in both hemispheres — it's just winter in the Southern Hemisphere. The show starts at 7 PM and ends at midnight.

Whether you get four or five shows a year depends on how many Sundays are in August. Every show plays out the same way, and you can do the boppers, raffle, and design fireworks all over again each Sunday. If you didn't win what you wanted this week, try again next week.

You'll see signs of the show during the day: Isabelle stands outside Resident Services all day, and her counter inside is closed. Able Sisters also stocks one yukata or kimono — the casual kimono, kabuki-actor yukata, or morning-glory yukata. If you want to watch the fireworks in a yukata, buy it during the day.

On show night, villagers gather in the plaza outside Resident Services (Image: Nookipedia)

02Isabelle: get a bopper, submit designs

Once the show starts, talk to Isabelle and she'll give you a light-up bopper — a random one of four: star, heart, flower, or bulb. If your inventory is full she can't hand it over, so free up a slot and go back to her before midnight.

Turning your own designs into fireworks

When you talk to Isabelle, pick the option about having a fireworks design, and you can choose patterns from your custom designs to give her.

You can submit up to 10 at a time and arrange them in order; any empty slots are filled with regular fireworks. Since you set the order, you can put the one you most want people to see first. If several designs form a set, place them next to each other and they'll go off as one continuous sequence. Designs submitted during the day launch as soon as the show starts at 7 PM; designs submitted after the show has started join the lineup right away. She stops taking submissions at midnight.

Submitted designs become fireworks and launch alongside the regular ones (Image: Nookipedia)

TipChoose designs with simple shapes and bright colors, and leave the background transparent — they'll be much easier to recognize in the sky. For a better view, push the right stick up to tilt the camera and fit the whole night sky on screen.

03Redd's raffle

Redd sets up a raffle booth in the plaza, but it only opens once the show starts. If you talk to him before 7 PM, he'll just say he's still getting ready.

Each pull costs 500 Bells, and there are no blanks — you always win something. If you're lucky, he'll even offer you another go. You can't play with a full inventory, so clear out your pockets before you head over.

Heads upPrizes are random, so if you've got your eye on one item, you'll just have to keep pulling. Bring enough Bells — ten pulls is 5,000.

04What to do with your prizes

Sparklers throw off sparks while you hold them. Get a few friends to line up and hold them together for a great photo.

Fountain fireworks go on the ground and shoot sparks upward once lit. Set one in front of your photo spot as a backdrop.

Balloons can be carried around, but if you trip, they'll float away, so watch your step.

Frozen treats, cotton candy, and boba are handheld props. Put on a yukata, grab one, and you're set for some summer-night photos.

TipYour friends' islands are having their fireworks shows on the same night, so you can visit each other and check out everyone's design fireworks. Come August, Nook Circle's Event Calendar lists every show.
