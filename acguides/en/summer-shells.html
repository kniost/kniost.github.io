Summer shell season: June through August, comb the beach

Three months of beach materials: how to collect them, where the DIY recipes come from, and a materials table for all 9 items

In summer, a kind of shell you never see the rest of the year washes up on the beach: summer shells. In the Northern Hemisphere they appear from June 1 to August 31, and in the Southern Hemisphere from December 1 to the end of February, the whole summer long.

Summer shells go into 9 DIY recipes, mostly ocean-themed walls and floors: the tropical vista, underwater wall, underwater flooring, and water flooring, plus the shell wreath, summer-shell rug, and more. Crafting all of them takes 36 summer shells, and a few also need regular shells and coral.

In the Southern Hemisphere, summer shell season spans the new year, running from December into the following February, and it overlaps with ornament season around Toy Day (December 15 to January 6).

02How to collect them

Summer shells work just like regular shells: they lie on the sand on the beach, and you just walk over and pick them up — no tools needed. Take a walk along the beach every day and grab them when you see them.

A summer shell sells for 600 Bells, quite a bit more than the 200-Bell seasonal materials like cherry-blossom petals and maple leaves, but if you plan to craft, hold on to them first. They stack up to 10 per pocket slot.

Keep some regular shells too

A few of this season's DIY recipes also need regular shells: the shell wreath needs 1 each of sea snail, sand dollar, coral, giant clam, and cowrie; the underwater flooring and underwater wall need 3 and 5 coral; and the shellfish pochette needs 2 giant clams. These shells show up on the beach all year round, so don't rush to sell them when you find them.

The starry-sands flooring also needs a sandy-beach flooring as its base, and the sandy-beach flooring has to be crafted first too.

TipSummer shells can go in storage. Stock up near the end of the season so you can keep crafting after it's over. And if you haven't learned every recipe for the season yet, no worries — you can pull them back out once you do.

03Where the DIY recipes come from

The big one is shooting down balloons. During summer shell season, balloons floating overhead drop this season's DIY recipes, and they'll keep doing so until you've learned them all.

Next is visiting Resident Services on the first day of the season. On June 1 in the north or December 1 in the south, Tom Nook or Isabelle will give you the shell wreath. You only get it on the first day.

The third is Kapp'n's boat tours, covered below.

Missed it? Catch up on Kapp'n's boat

Kapp'n at the pier will take you out to sea once a day for 1,000 Nook Miles. Every so often, he'll drop you on an island stuck in a particular season, and the summer shell island is one of them; each seasonal island has about a 3% chance. On that island you can pick up summer shells on the beach, and message bottles on the shore may hold this season's DIY recipes.

The odds are low, so this is really just luck. There's also a catch: your own island has to have gone through this season at least once before you can be taken to the matching island. Once you've decorated your hotel's first VIP room, Kapp'n will take you on an extra trip each day (for 100 hotel tickets), which gives you a slightly better shot.

05What to make first

Shell wreath: the one you get on the first day. It's a ring of shells you hang on the wall, and it needs one each of 6 kinds of shell. Collecting them all takes a while, so start early in the season.

Underwater wall + underwater flooring: a matching wall and floor that turn your room into the seabed. Together they take 6 summer shells and 8 coral.

Tropical vista, water flooring: these only need summer shells (5 and 6), nothing else, so they're the easiest to put together.

Summer-shell rug: 6 summer shells, and it looks good by the sea or indoors.

Shell wand: this season's wand. Wave it to instantly change into an outfit you've saved.
