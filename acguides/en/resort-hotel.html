The Ver. 3.0 resort hotel: earning and spending hotel tickets

Unlock conditions, decorating rooms, the souvenir shop, and VIP rooms

The free Ver. 3.0 update adds a resort hotel next to the pier, run by Kapp'n's family. In the game it's simply called "the hotel," so that's what we'll call it here. It plays a lot like Happy Home Paradise: you decorate rooms for the guests who stay, earn hotel tickets, then spend them at the souvenir shop on furniture and clothing you can't get anywhere else.

Hotel tickets can only be spent at the souvenir shop, and Bells are no good there, so how many tickets you earn each day basically decides how quickly you can bring home what you want. The single most important step is to finish decorating all 8 standard guest rooms as soon as possible: VIP rooms, a second boat trip, and new shop stock are all gated behind it. Below we'll go through unlocking, decorating, earning tickets, and spending them, in that order.

The hotel at the pier, with Kapp'n's whole family out front (Image: Nintendo)

01How to unlock it, and who's who

The hotel appears only once you meet all three conditions: a 3-star island rating, K.K. Slider's first concert on your island, and a finished museum building. Once all three are done, the pier is expanded and the hotel appears. If you already met all three when you updated, the pier will already be expanded.

On your first visit, Tom Nook asks you to help out at the front desk by decorating a guest room. The first one is always the Seaside Room, and a guest checks in the same day you finish it. Afterward, talk to Tom Nook again to hold an opening ceremony.

Kapp'n's family runs the hotel, each with their own job. Here's who to talk to for what:

02Decorating guest rooms

There are 8 standard guest rooms upstairs. Talk to Leilani at the front desk to take on a job. You can decorate up to 2 per day, earning 200 hotel tickets each. At two a day without skipping, you'll finish all 8 in 4 days.

The first room is always the Seaside Room. From the second room on, Leilani offers two themes each time and you pick one. There are 15 standard room themes in total:

Seaside, Japanese, Modern, Simple, Ranch, Kiddie, Dreamy, Skyscraper, Diner, Lodge, Japanese-Retro, Exquisite, Frozen, Luxurious, and Antique.

A finished Seaside Room (Image: Nintendo)

When you're done, remember to snap a photo with your NookPhone camera. It gets hung on the floor map with the designer's and guest's names next to it. If you own Happy Home Paradise, any features you've unlocked there — like partition walls, lighting adjustments, and room resizing — work here too.

Once a room is decorated, guests check in every day, and you can drop in to chat with them. When all 8 rooms are booked, only one guest of each personality type will stay.

03How to earn hotel tickets

The souvenir shop only takes hotel tickets — Bells won't buy anything there — so you'll have to save them up bit by bit. Here's how to earn them:

The last three rows only open up after you've decorated your first VIP room. Once all 8 standard rooms are done, there are no new rooms to take on, so from then on your steady daily income comes from VIP rooms, redecorating, and drop box requests.

DIY drop box

There's a drop box on the pier with 4 requests posted at a time. Each one spells out what to craft, how many to turn in, and how many tickets you'll get. Fill one and a new one takes its place. The whole batch is replaced every Monday, and any unfinished requests expire. Besides tickets, filling requests also raises the hotel's popularity, which later decides when the retro consoles arrive in the shop.

The DIY drop box: each request shows what to turn in, how many, and how many tickets it pays (Image: Nintendo)

TipWhen crafting at a workbench in your own home, you can pull materials straight from storage and craft up to 10 at once. Requests often ask for several of the same item, so craft them all at home in one go before heading over — it saves a lot of running back and forth.

04What to buy at the souvenir shop

Grams runs the souvenir shop, and there are two ways to buy from her.

One is to buy what's on display. Two items on the shelves change every day, priced 30% below the catalog, and you take them home as soon as you pay.

The other is to order from the catalog. The catalog has everything, but at full price, and orders arrive the next day.

So glance at the shelves whenever you pass by and grab anything you want while it's cheap; order everything else from the catalog.

The marble, artful, and tubular series are sold only here. There's also the hotel mug, hotel pennant, hotel snow globe, and hotel wall clock, plus T-shirts, aloha shirts, umbrellas, and more with the hotel logo.

The catalog isn't complete from the start. A new batch of items is added once you've decorated 4 rooms, and another at 8. Sometimes they take an extra day to show up; a red dot appears on the catalog when there's new stock.

The final batch is Nintendo's retro consoles: the Famicom, Game Boy, Super Famicom, and others at 500 tickets each; classic toys like the Love Tester and Ultra Hand at 200; and the Nintendo Switch 2 at 222. These only arrive after you've decorated your first VIP room and the hotel is popular enough. Popularity mainly goes up by decorating VIP rooms and filling DIY requests, so keep doing both if you want to see these sooner.

05VIP rooms and amiibo guests

VIP rooms open the day after you finish all 8 standard rooms. There are 45 VIP themes in total: all 15 standard themes, plus 30 exclusive to VIP rooms.

You have to invite a guest before you can start decorating a VIP room. Guests stay for just one night and check out the next day, clearing the room, so you can do a fresh one every day — 1 per day, 200 hotel tickets each. While decorating, press the left stick to show or hide the guest so they don't block your view while placing furniture.

What opens up after your first VIP room

Finishing your first VIP room unlocks a bunch of new things at once:

· An amiibo scanner appears at the front desk. Each day you can scan an amiibo to invite a guest from off the island to stay in a VIP room.

· You can redecorate finished standard rooms once a day, switching to any of the 15 themes, or going with no theme at all.

· You can take a second trip on Kapp'n's boat each day; the second trip costs 100 hotel tickets.

· Tourists who can't find a place to stay start showing up on your island. Take them to the hotel for tickets.

NoteYou can't invite villagers who live on your island or a visitor currently at your campsite. Tom Nook, Isabelle, Blathers, Timmy, Tommy, Sable, Mabel, Wilbur, Orville, Resetti, and Kapp'n's family can't be invited either. Visitors like Saharah and C.J. can't be invited on a day they happen to be on your island — just try again another day.

06A few tips

Finish all 8 rooms first. New shop stock, VIP rooms, and the second boat trip all unlock off this step, and the sooner you finish, the more tickets you can earn each day. Don't skip your two rooms a day early on.

Check the sale items daily, save up for the big stuff. The 30%-off shelf items change every day, so take a look whenever you pass by. Save up for 500-ticket pieces like the marble fireplace and order them from the catalog. If you don't like the color of something you bought, Cyrus on Harv's Island can customize it.

Dress the mannequins in the lobby. On your second day working at the hotel, Leilani introduces you to the two mannequins in the lobby. Dress them up and guests will copy their outfits, and friends who visit can borrow them too. Clothes you put on them aren't taken out of your storage, so pick freely.

Turn in requests before Monday. The drop box refreshes every Monday, so turn in any requests you can craft by Sunday before they expire.
