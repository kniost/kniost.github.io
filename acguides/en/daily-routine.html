Your daily island routine

A daily checklist, the fixed days of the week, and a route with no backtracking

A batch of things refreshes on your island every day: buried fossils, rocks full of materials, new stock at the shop — they all reset at 5 AM. The game's day starts at 5 AM, so if you log on at 1 AM, the game still counts it as the previous day. The new day only kicks in after 5.

The daily to-do list looks scattered, but most of it you can grab on a single loop of the island. Then just remember three fixed days each week: turnips on Sunday morning, new hotel requests on Monday, and K.K. on Saturday night. With a smooth route — and empty pockets before you hit the shops — you can usually wrap it all up in 20–30 minutes.

01What to pick up on your loop

Grab your shovel and take a lap around the island. You'll find these every day.

Fossils

Star-shaped cracks in the ground hide fossils — 4 a day. Dig them up and take them to Blathers at the museum for assessment first. Donate the ones the museum is missing and keep duplicates to sell.

Take the fossils you dig up to Blathers at the museum for assessment (Image: Nintendo)

Rocks

Every rock on your island gives stone, clay, and iron nuggets each day, with the occasional gold nugget. One of them is a money rock that gives Bells. Dig a few holes behind you to block your escape route before hitting it, so you don't get knocked back. That way you can get 8 hits in a row and collect everything a rock has in one go.

Glowing spot

Every day there's one glowing spot in the ground — dig it up for a bag of Bells. Don't fill the hole right away: bury Bells in it to plant a money tree, which grows into a tree bearing three bags of the same amount. Players generally find that burying 10,000 Bells or less is the safe bet.

Message bottle

A message bottle washes up on the beach every day with a DIY recipe inside. Pick it up as you walk along the shore.

Balloons

Every now and then a balloon with a present floats by. Pull out your slingshot and pop it — you might get Bells, materials, or furniture.

02Villagers and visitors

Chat with your villagers every day. Your friendship grows over time, and you'll occasionally get gifts or be asked to help out with small favors.

Each day one villager stays home crafting at their workbench — go in and chat to learn a new recipe. Pop into each villager's house as you pass to find the one who's crafting.

Almost every weekday brings a special visitor: Saharah selling rugs, C.J. buying fish, Flick buying bugs, or Leif selling flower starts. If you find Gulliver passed out on the beach, wake him up and help him find his communicator parts, and he'll mail you a gift later. For how to make the most of each visitor, see "Weekly visitors: who comes when, and what they do."

If someone's staying at the campsite, go say hi too. If you have an open plot, you can invite them to move in.

03Make the rounds at the shops

Nook's Cranny swaps in new furniture and DIY recipes every day. The sign out front shows the day's hot item, which sells for double that day. If you happen to have one, or enough materials to craft one, sell it that same day.

Able Sisters restocks clothes, shoes, and hats every day. In the changing room you can try on and buy styles that aren't on display that day, so if you're hunting for something specific, don't just check the racks.

On the Nook Stop at Resident Services, Nook Shopping has a Daily Selection that changes every day. Order anything you're missing from your catalog — it arrives the next day. While you're there, tap your finished tasks in Nook Miles+ to claim your miles.

Able Sisters closes at 9 PM and the shop at 10 PM. If you only get to play in the evening, hit the shops first and leave the gathering for later.

04The pier and the hotel

Once your island hits 3 stars, Kapp'n's boat docks at the pier. Each day you can pay 1,000 Nook Miles for a trip to another island, where the season and weather may differ from yours.

If you've opened the hotel, add these to your daily run:

While the 8 guest rooms aren't all decorated yet, you can decorate up to 2 per day, earning 200 hotel tickets each. Use both slots every day to finish the rooms sooner.

The DIY drop box at the pier lists 4 requests. Craft the items and drop them in to earn hotel tickets.

The Hotel Souvenir Shop puts two pieces of furniture on sale every day. Take a peek when you pass by.

Once you unlock the VIP rooms, you can decorate one per day.

For everything about the hotel, see "The Ver. 3.0 resort hotel: earning and spending hotel tickets."

05Shooting stars at night, and the weekly rhythm

On clear nights, shooting stars sometimes streak across the sky. Put away your tools, look up, and press A to make a wish — the next morning you'll find star fragments on the beach, which you can use to craft zodiac furniture and wands. On meteor shower days, Isabelle announces it in the morning, and Celeste visits the island too.

Want to know in advance which nights — and what times — shooting stars will appear? Enter your island seed in Nook Circle's Weather forecast.

Besides the daily stuff, there are a few fixed days each week. Miss one and you'll have to wait another week:

WhenWhat to do
Sunday morningDaisy Mae sells turnips; she leaves at noon
Monday–SaturdayThe shop buys turnips, with one price in the morning and one in the afternoon
MondayAll the hotel drop box requests refresh

NoteTurnips bought on Sunday spoil by the next Sunday, so sell them before the shop closes on Saturday.

06A time-saving route

Every island's layout is different, so you can't copy a route exactly, but the idea is the same: empty your pockets first and avoid backtracking. Tweak this order to fit your island.

1. Listen to Isabelle's announcement as you head out to find out who's visiting and whether there's a meteor shower tonight.

2. Grab your shovel and loop along the coast: dig up fossils, hit rocks, pick up the message bottle, and dig up the glowing spot.

3. Pop into villagers' houses as you pass to find the one who's crafting; if there's a visitor in the plaza, swing by to say hi.

4. Get your fossils assessed at the museum, then head to the shop to sell what's in your pockets and check the hot item, then browse Able Sisters.

5. At Resident Services, check the Daily Selection on the Nook Stop, claim your miles, and deposit your Bells in the ATM.

6. Finish at the pier and the hotel: decorate rooms, drop off DIY items, and browse the souvenir shop.

The things you do every day are exactly the ones you're most likely to forget. Nook Circle has a Daily Checklist — tick things off and you'll see at a glance what's done.

Of course, none of this is mandatory. If you just feel like fishing one day, go fish. Your island isn't going anywhere.
