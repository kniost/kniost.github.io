Young spring bamboo season: late February to late May, take an axe to your bamboo

How to get young spring bamboo, where the DIY recipes come from, and a materials table for all 12 bamboo items

Every spring, the bamboo on your island starts sprouting fresh young spring bamboo. In the Northern Hemisphere it runs from February 25 to May 31, and in the Southern Hemisphere from August 25 to November 30. That's over three months, one of the roomier material seasons, so there's no need to rush.

Young spring bamboo goes into a batch of very bamboo-flavored furniture: the bamboo noodle slide, the bamboo-shoot lamp, the steamer-basket set, the bamboo-grove wall, and even a basket pack you can wear. There are 12 DIY recipes this season, and crafting all of them takes 62 young spring bamboo. You'll need bamboo on your island first, though; if you don't have any, section 02 explains how to get some.

Once your bamboo fills out into a grove, you'll have young spring bamboo to harvest every spring (Image: Nookipedia)

01When

All dates are in-game dates. The Southern Hemisphere's seasons are half a year off from the north's, so everything shifts by 6 months.

Cherry-blossom season (April 1–10 in the north, October 1–10 in the south) falls right in the middle of bamboo season. For those 10 days you can harvest young spring bamboo and catch cherry-blossom petals at the same time, so don't let either slip by.

02How to get it

Take your axe and hit some bamboo. During the season, hitting bamboo gives you young spring bamboo. It only sells for 200 Bells apiece, so it's not worth selling; you'll get more out of it as furniture.

Young spring bamboo stacks up to 30 per pocket slot. If you end up with a lot, put some in storage to free up your pockets and keep going.

No bamboo on your island?

Not every island starts out with bamboo. One kind of mystery island you can fly to with a Nook Miles Ticket is covered in bamboo. Dig up bamboo shoots next to the bamboo with a shovel, bring them home, and plant them, and they'll grow into bamboo.

Bamboo shoots are a material too: this season's bamboo-grove wall needs 3 and the bamboo-shoot lamp needs 5. Once the bamboo on your own island has grown in, you can dig up shoots next to it as well, so remember to keep a few.

TipYoung spring bamboo can go in storage. Stock up near the end of the season so you can keep crafting after it's over. And if you haven't learned every recipe for the season yet, no worries — you can pull it back out once you do.

03Where the DIY recipes come from

Having young spring bamboo isn't enough — you need to learn the DIY recipes before you can craft anything. There are three sources.

The big one is shooting down balloons. During bamboo season, balloons floating overhead drop this season's DIY recipes, and they'll keep doing so until you've learned them all. The season lasts three months, so just pop balloons whenever you see them and you'll fill out the set over time.

Next is visiting Resident Services on the first day of the season. On February 25 in the north or August 25 in the south, Tom Nook or Isabelle will give you the bamboo noodle slide. You only get it on the first day, so pop into Resident Services first thing when you log in.

The third is Kapp'n's boat tours, covered below.

Missed it? Catch up on Kapp'n's boat

Kapp'n at the pier will take you out to sea once a day for 1,000 Nook Miles. Every so often, he'll drop you on an island stuck in a particular season, and the bamboo island is one of them; each seasonal island has about a 3% chance. On that island you can harvest young spring bamboo, and message bottles on the beach may hold this season's DIY recipes.

The odds are low, so this is really just luck. There's also a catch: your own island has to have gone through this season at least once before you can be taken to the matching island. Once you've decorated your hotel's first VIP room, Kapp'n will take you on an extra trip each day (for 100 hotel tickets), which gives you a slightly better shot.

Crafting all 12 takes 62 young spring bamboo, 8 bamboo shoots, 10 weeds, 4 clay, 3 wood, and 3 star fragments in total.

05What to make first

Bamboo noodle slide: the one you get on the first day. It's a 3-tile-long bamboo trough that gives your yard a real Japanese-garden feel.

Bamboo-shoot lamp: needs 4 young spring bamboo, 5 bamboo shoots, and 4 clay. You have to dig up the bamboo shoots yourself, which is one more step than the others, so make it early in the season.

Basket pack: a bag you can wear on your back. Just 6 young spring bamboo, and it's the perfect accessory for spring outings.

Bamboo wand: this season's wand. Wave it to instantly change into an outfit you've saved ahead of time. It also takes 3 star fragments.

The three bamboo mats and rugs (light bamboo rug, light bamboo bath mat, yellow bamboo mat) all need very few materials and work well indoors or in your yard.
