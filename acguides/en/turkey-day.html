Turkey Day: gather ingredients for Franklin's four dishes

The fourth Thursday of November every year — and you can prep the ingredients a month ahead

On Turkey Day, long tables are set up in the plaza in front of Resident Services, and your villagers show up in hats, plates in hand, to celebrate. The chef is Franklin the turkey: he does the cooking, and you run around finding ingredients. It runs from 9 AM to midnight on the fourth Thursday of November every year; check the Event Calendar in Nook Circle for the exact date.

Franklin makes four dishes, and each one you help finish gets you a piece of Turkey Day furniture: the Turkey Day rug, wall, and flooring, and finally the cornucopia. For each dish he'll also ask for a "secret ingredient" that earns an extra reward, and the secret ingredient for the fourth dish gets you DIY recipes for the whole Turkey Day set. His list includes pumpkins and seafood — some need diving, some need pumpkins that take time to grow — so scrambling on the day is stressful. Plant pumpkins ahead of time and stash seafood in storage, and the day itself will be a breeze.

01When it happens

The date follows American Thanksgiving: the fourth Thursday of November every year, on the same day in both hemispheres. In the Northern Hemisphere it celebrates the fall harvest; in the Southern Hemisphere it's spring, so the celebration has a spring theme and some of Franklin's ingredients are different.

02How the day works

It goes one dish at a time:

1. After 9 AM, find Franklin in the plaza, and he'll tell you what the current dish is missing.

2. Hand him the ingredients, the dish is done, and you get that dish's reward.

3. He'll then ask whether you'd like to add a "secret ingredient." Bring it and you get a bonus reward.

4. Once all four dishes are done, your villagers dance and sing in front of Resident Services, partying until midnight.

Here's what each dish and its secret ingredient call for. Where it says "one of," Franklin picks one item from the list to ask for — just bring whichever he names that day.

Missing something? Visit your villagers

If you're short on ingredients, drop by your villagers' homes. Villagers cooking at home will be missing a fish or sea creature. Bring it to them, and they'll give you one of Franklin's ingredients in return — and let slip what the current secret ingredient is. If you're not sure about the secret ingredient, make the rounds first before you go hunting, and you'll save yourself some wasted trips.

The big one is Cozy Turkey Day DIY from the fourth dish, which teaches you 8 DIY recipes at once: Turkey Day hearth, Turkey Day table, Turkey Day chair, Turkey Day table setting, Turkey Day casserole, Turkey Day decorations, Turkey Day garden stand, and Turkey Day wheat decor. In the Northern Hemisphere you learn the fall harvest colors; in the Southern Hemisphere, the spring colors — and once crafted, you can customize between the two. Skipping the secret ingredient for the first three dishes only costs you a random piece of furniture, but don't miss the fourth.

After all four dishes, Franklin also gives you Turkey Day Recipes, which teaches you the cooking recipes for all four dishes. You need to have unlocked cooking first to learn them.

04What to prep a month ahead

Plant pumpkins early. Leif sells pumpkin starts year-round for 280 Bells each, and the color they grow into is random. The pumpkin pie needs orange plus one other color, and the secret ingredient takes the other two, so keep a few of all four colors on hand and you'll have whatever Franklin asks for.

Catch seafood ahead of time and store it. Dig up manila clams on the beach, dive for scallops and Dungeness crabs, and fish for barred knifejaws and sea bass off the coast. All of these can go in storage, so keep them whenever you come across them and you won't have to scour the ocean on the day.

Keep a few bags of flour. The olive-flounder meunière might call for flour. Grow your own wheat, or buy it at Nook's Cranny.

TipMissed the day? You can still catch up: from the day after Turkey Day through November 30, Nook's Cranny stocks both Cozy Turkey Day DIY and Turkey Day Recipes. It lines up with the Nook Friday sale, when everything is 30% off — 13,860 Bells for the DIY set and 6,860 Bells for the cooking recipes.

05Version changes

Starting with Ver. 2.0, the gratin may call for carrots or potatoes, and the olive-flounder meunière may call for flour. The same version added Turkey Day Recipes and the Nook Friday sale after Turkey Day. The Ver. 3.0 patch notes don't mention any Turkey Day changes.
