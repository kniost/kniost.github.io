International Museum Day: collect stamps at the museum and trade them to Blathers for plaques

May 18 to 31 every year: four exhibits, four stamp cards

International Museum Day is a stamp rally Blathers runs at the museum, from May 18 to 31 every year, on the same dates in both hemispheres. During the event, each of the four exhibits — bugs, fish, fossils, and art — has a few stamp stands. Fill up the card for an exhibit and Blathers will give you that exhibit's plaque. You can't buy the plaques anywhere; this event is the only way to get them.

There's nothing to prepare — just stroll through the museum while the event is on. The real draw is that you can do a fresh round every day: each plaque sells for 2,500 Bells, so going every day for 14 days adds up to a nice pile of Bells. If your museum doesn't have an art gallery yet, you can't do the art card — more on that below.

Blathers in the lobby: the museum is holding a stamp rally for International Museum Day (Image: Nookipedia)

01When it happens

The dates are fixed: May 18 to May 31 every year, 14 days total, the same in both hemispheres. The real-world International Museum Day is May 18, so the game kicks off that day and keeps going for two weeks.

Island announcements will remind you both before and during the event. The museum is open around the clock, so if you're busy during the day, you can collect stamps at night just as well.

02How it works

It's quick. Just follow these steps:

· Step 1: Go into the museum and talk to Blathers in the lobby. He'll explain the rules.

· Step 2: Look for the little stamp stands in the bug, fish, fossil, and art exhibits — there are 3 in each. Walk up and press A to stamp your card.

· Step 3: Once you have all 3 stamps for an exhibit, head back to the lobby and talk to Blathers. He'll add a completion stamp to your card and give you that exhibit's plaque.

· Step 4: After you turn in all four, Blathers will congratulate you once more, and that's it for the day.

The stamp stands move every day, but they only rotate among a fixed set of spots. Here's how many possible spots each exhibit has, along with a few of the easier ones to check:

ExhibitSpotsEasy ones to check
Bugs11South of the butterfly room, right of the big tree, the wooden bridge behind the waterfall
Fish10In front of the small tanks, in front of the pond, in the middle of the coral reef, the deep-sea area with the coelacanth
Fossils7Either side of the entrance, in front of the Diplodocus, in front of the Plesiosaurus
Art4By the statues, the left side of the back hall, near the Eastern and Western artwork

The bug exhibit has the most possible spots (11), so it takes the most searching. The art gallery only has 4, so you'll usually find them with one lap.

03What you can get

Each completed card gets you the plaque for that exhibit. All four sell for the same price:

CardRewardSells for (Bells)
Bugsbug plaque2,500
Fishfish plaque2,500
Fossilsfossil plaque2,500
Artart plaque2,500

The plaques are wall-mounted furniture you can't buy in any shop — this event is the only source. Hang all four on one wall and you've got a matching set.

04Quick tips

Go one exhibit at a time. Fill one card, take it back to Blathers in the lobby, then move on to the next exhibit. You'll usually spot the stamp stands with one lap of an exhibit; check the common spots in the table above first, and only search more carefully if they're not there.

You can do another round every day. The next day the stands move, but completing the cards still gets you the same 4 plaques. Sell the ones you don't need: that's up to 4 plaques and 10,000 Bells a day, or up to 140,000 Bells if you go all 14 days.

If your museum doesn't have an art gallery yet, you can't complete the art card. You'll need to buy a genuine piece of art from Redd and donate it to Blathers before the art gallery gets built. The event only lasts two weeks, so if you can make it in time, take care of that first.

Display all four plaques together. They look great in a museum-themed room. Keep one set for yourself and sell the extras.

05Version history

This event was added in the 1.2.0 update in 2020, when only the bug, fish, and fossil exhibits had stamp stands. The 1.10.0 update in 2021 added stamp stands to the art gallery and raised the plaques' sell price from 300 to 2,500 Bells. Neither the 2.0 nor the 3.0 patch notes mention the event.
