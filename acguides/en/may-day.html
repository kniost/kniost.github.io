May Day maze tour: one ticket, one path to Rover

April 29 to May 7 every year, one trip per year

Tom Nook says May Day is a day to thank hardworking folks, so he's arranged a trip for every resident. The destination is a little island laid out as a hedge maze, and waiting at the end is Rover, the blue-and-purple cat.

The tour is open from April 29 to May 7 every year, the same in both hemispheres, and you only get one trip per year. You arrive with empty pockets, so clearing the path depends entirely on the shovel, fruit, and materials you find in the maze. Be especially careful with your fruit, or you'll end up stuck behind a tree halfway through. Reach the end and you'll get Rover's briefcase on your first visit and Rover's photo on your second, and you can grab up to 9 Bell vouchers along the way. There's nothing to prepare — just pick a free day and go. Below, we'll walk through getting there, solving the maze, and collecting your rewards.

During the event, talk to Tom Nook in Resident Services and he'll tell you about the May Day tour (Image: Nookipedia)

01When you can go

The dates are fixed: April 29 to May 7, every year. It doesn't move with Bunny Day and doesn't differ by hemisphere.

Any day in that 9-day window works, so there's no need to rush on day one. But once you finish and come home, you can't go again until next year, so pick a day when you're not in a hurry and take your time.

02Talk to Tom Nook, then Orville

Getting there takes two stops:

1. Go to Resident Services and talk to Tom Nook. He'll tell you he's already booked your ticket.

2. Head to the airport and tell Orville at the counter you want to fly out.

3. When you land on the maze island, your pockets will be empty — none of your own tools come with you.

It works the other way too: nothing you pick up on the maze island comes home with you. The only things you actually keep are Rover's gift and the Bell vouchers, and both get mailed to you.

03Getting through the maze

The maze is walled in by hedges, and the path is blocked here and there by trees, rocks, and bushes. Each obstacle has its own fix.

Bushes are the easiest: just dig them up with the shovel.

Trees and rocks require eating a piece of fruit first to power up. Then you can dig a tree out by the roots with the shovel, or smash a rock. One piece of fruit is good for one use only — dig up one tree or break one rock and the power is gone.

The worn axe can only chop down a single tree before it breaks, so save it for the one that matters most.

Partway through there's a simple DIY workbench. Learn any DIY recipes you find in the maze, then craft the tools here once you've gathered the materials.

The maze comes in two layouts, and which one you get may change from year to year:

LayoutWhat's different
FlatThe whole island is level, and some small puddles can only be vaulted straight across; you'll need to gather materials to craft an axe
CliffTwo levels; collect wood, hardwood, and softwood to craft a ladder and climb the cliff

Flat layout: dig up trees to clear your way (Image: Nintendo, via Nookipedia)

Cliff layout: craft a ladder to climb up — Rover is in the top right (Image: Nintendo, via Nookipedia)

Heads upIf you take a wrong turn, run out of fruit, or get stuck somewhere, open the Rescue Service app on your NookPhone. For 100 Nook Miles, you'll be sent back to the start and the whole maze resets. Anything you picked up on the island is cleared, but a gift you've already received from Rover isn't affected.

04What you can get

When you reach the end, Rover gives you a gift that depends on how many times you've made it there. There are also Bell vouchers scattered around the maze, some of them tucked behind rocks that take a little extra effort.

ItemHow to get it
Rover's briefcaseReach the end for the first time
Rover's photoReach the end again the following year
Bell vouchers4 near the end, plus 5 more behind 3 rocks along the island's edge

The gift and the vouchers both arrive in your mailbox the next day, so don't panic if they're not in your pockets when you get home. Each Bell voucher sells for 3,000 Bells, so all 9 add up to 27,000 Bells.

05Things to keep in mind before you go

Don't waste fruit. The 5 vouchers on the edge of the island are blocked by 3 rocks, and you'll need fruit to break them. Before digging up any tree, ask yourself whether you really have to.

Look before you act. The maze isn't big. Walk around it once first to see which trees actually block the path and which rocks are hiding something, then decide where your fruit goes.

Learn DIY recipes as soon as you find them. The ladder and axe recipes are both hidden in the maze, and you can't craft either without learning it first. Read them as soon as you pick them up.

Check in with Tom Nook when you get back. He'll give you a pat on the back, and that officially wraps up this year's tour.

At the end, Rover is waiting for you by the campfire (Image: Nookipedia)

06Version history

May Day was added in the 1.2.0 update in 2020. Back then it ran from May 1 to 7, and there was only the flat layout. The 1.10.0 update in 2021 moved the start date up to April 29, added the cliff layout and Rover's photo, and changed the gift and vouchers to arrive by mail the next day. Since 2.0, either layout can appear depending on the year. Nothing changed in 3.0.
