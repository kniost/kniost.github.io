Bunny Day: where to find all six eggs and how to collect every DIY recipe

Starts the week before Easter and runs 8 days, with two visits from Zipper

Bunny Day runs for 8 days: you spend the first 7 hunting eggs all over the island, and the 8th is Bunny Day itself, which always falls on a Sunday. Six kinds of eggs pop up during the event, and each comes from somewhere different: digging, shaking trees, popping balloons, hitting rocks, fishing, and chopping trees. The host, Zipper, shows up twice — on the first day to hand out your task, and on the last day to see how you did.

There are a lot of DIY recipes to collect: 15 for furniture and 18 for clothing. Furniture recipes come from popping balloons, message bottles, and villagers, while clothing recipes come from collecting enough of each egg type. On Bunny Day, give Zipper 1 of each of the six eggs to get the Bunny Day basket. If you've crafted all the Bunny Day furniture, he'll also teach you two special DIY recipes. So during the first 7 days, set aside a few of each egg instead of crafting them all into furniture.

01When it happens

Bunny Day lines up with real-world Easter, so the date changes every year: the first Sunday after the first full moon following the spring equinox (March 21), which always lands between March 22 and April 25. Both hemispheres share the same date — it's just already fall in the Southern Hemisphere.

The event kicks off on the Sunday a week before Easter. The first 7 days are for egg hunting, and the 8th day is Bunny Day itself. To see this year's exact dates, check the Event Calendar in Nook Circle.

02When Zipper visits

The event is hosted by Zipper, who comes by twice.

On the first day, he wanders around the island. Talk to him and he'll teach you the Bunny Day bed DIY recipe and ask you to craft all the Bunny Day furniture. If you miss him that day, no worries — the recipe will be mailed to you afterward. If you already know the bed recipe, he'll give you a random one you don't have yet.

On Bunny Day itself, he waits in front of Resident Services for you to bring him eggs (more on that in section 05). Your villagers dress up in Bunny Day outfits that day too.

During the egg hunt, Nook's Cranny also stocks 5 Bunny Day items: Bunny Day candy, Bunny Day garden flag, Bunny Day planter box, Bunny Day topiary, and Bunny Day tree.

03Where to find each egg

Each egg type has its own source. If you just go about your usual island routine, you'll run into pretty much all of them:

EggHow to get it
Earth eggDig up star-shaped cracks in the ground with a shovel; up to 10 a day during the hunt, up to 15 on Bunny Day
Leaf eggShake hardwood trees
Sky eggShoot down Bunny Day–themed balloons with a slingshot; only on your own island
Stone eggHit rocks with a shovel or axe
Water eggRandomly hooked when fishing medium-sized shadows in rivers, ponds, or the sea
Wood eggChop trees with an axe; palm trees drop more

Except for sky eggs, which only appear on your own island, every egg type shows up more often on mystery islands. If you're short on one kind at home, take a trip to stock up.

04Collecting every DIY recipe

15 furniture recipes

There are 15 furniture recipes in total: 11 pieces of furniture, plus a wallpaper, flooring, rug, and fence. You'll pick them up gradually from a few sources: shooting down Bunny Day balloons, checking message bottles that wash up on the beach, or getting them from villagers. Hit all of these every day during the hunt and you'll fill out the set much faster.

18 clothing recipes

There are 18 clothing recipes: a shell, an outfit, and shoes for each of the six egg types. Once you've collected enough of a given egg, you'll have a flash of inspiration and come up with the clothing recipe for that egg. So you don't need anyone to give you these — just keep collecting eggs. Learn all 18 and you'll also get two more: the egg party hat and the egg party dress.

The arch and the wobbling Zipper toy eat up the most eggs — 2 and 4 of each type except water eggs, respectively. If you want either one, stockpile extra during the hunt.

05Visiting Zipper on Bunny Day

On Bunny Day, find Zipper in front of Resident Services. First, give him 1 of each of the six eggs, and he'll give you the Bunny Day basket in return. After that, he'll also swap eggs for you: 3 of the same kind gets you 1 egg of your choice. It's a handy way to even out your surplus and shortfalls.

If you've crafted all 15 furniture items plus the Bunny Day bag and Bunny Day crown — 17 in total, he'll teach you the wobbling Zipper toy DIY recipe that day. Once you craft the toy, you'll also get the Bunny Day wand recipe, which takes 1 wobbling Zipper toy and 3 star fragments. The bag and crown recipes only come from chatting with villagers, so don't miss those two.

From the second year on, Zipper just gives you a Bunny Day wand outright.

06Quick tips

Water eggs are the easiest to get and the least useful. They can turn up any time you fish a medium-sized shadow, but the only things that use them are the stool, the Bunny Day festive balloons, and the three water-egg clothing items. Keep enough for those and sell the rest.

Short on eggs? Visit a mystery island. Stone eggs and wood eggs are plentiful there, so one Nook Miles Ticket can go a long way.

Don't rush to sell eggs on Bunny Day. Zipper's 3-for-1 swap lets you turn your extras into the kind you're missing. You can sell whatever's left afterward.

Don't shoot balloons over water. If a balloon drops into the water, it's gone for good. Wait until it drifts over land before you fire.

07Version history

Starting with 1.9.0, the event was shortened from 12 days to 8, and Nook's Cranny began selling the 5 Bunny Day items. Since 2.0, Bunny Day candy is edible, and eating it powers you up so you can dig up trees and break rocks. The 3.0 patch notes don't mention Bunny Day.
