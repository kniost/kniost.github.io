Wedding Season: help Reese and Cyrus with their wedding photos on Harv's Island

Runs all of June: one photo shoot a day, and heart crystals to trade for wedding furniture

Wedding Season stars Reese and Cyrus, the alpaca couple who run Re-Tail. They want to redo their wedding photos at Harvey's Photopia studio and need your help setting the scene. You can do one shoot every day in June. Each shoot earns you wedding furniture plus heart crystals, which you can save up and trade to Cyrus for even more wedding furniture.

There's no rush with this event; the hard part is sticking with it. The wedding furniture only fully unlocks after 7 shoots, and buying everything takes around 260 heart crystals, so you'll be chipping away one shoot a day. Starting any time before mid-June is fine, but the earlier you start, the more breathing room you'll have. Below, we'll cover how the event starts and how each day's shoot works, then how to spend your crystals wisely.

01When and where

The dates are fixed: June 1 to June 30 every year, all day long. It's in June for the Southern Hemisphere too — it doesn't flip with the seasons, so everyone starts and ends on the same day.

HemisphereDates
NorthernJune 1–30 every year
SouthernJune 1–30 every year (not flipped)

The first time you step outside on June 1, Harvey will call you for help. Talk to Orville at the airport and choose Harv's Island. When you arrive, you'll see Harvey, Reese, and Cyrus chatting outside the studio. Go talk to them and the event begins.

02How each day's shoot works

The routine is the same every day, and you'll have it down after one run.

1. Talk to Reese first. She'll describe the scene she wants that day. The first day is the wedding ceremony, to be decorated in white and pink.

2. Go into the room and decorate. You'll be using wedding furniture Cyrus has on hand, so there's no need to bring anything from your island.

3. When you're done, take out your camera, snap a photo of the couple, and show it to Reese.

4. If Reese is happy, she'll give you a piece of wedding furniture and some heart crystals. Hang on to the crystals and trade them to Cyrus for other wedding furniture.

You get one shoot per day, with a new theme each time. The more wedding furniture you place, and the better it fits the theme, the more heart crystals you get — anywhere from a few to over a dozen. So don't just plop down two items and call it a day; fill the space as much as you can with her theme in mind.

Day 6 is a bit different: it's the reception. This time you can invite villagers into the set as guests, and there's no photo to take. Afterward, you'll receive the Reese & Cyrus photo plate.

Before your 7th shoot, Harvey gives you the wedding fence DIY recipe. After the 7th shoot, Cyrus gives you the wedding wand DIY recipe, and all the remaining wedding furniture unlocks, so you'll suddenly have a lot more to trade for.

03What heart crystals can buy

Take your heart crystals to Cyrus to trade for furniture. He'll sell you up to 8 items a day, and your purchases are delivered to your home the next day. Prices (in heart crystals) are below:

ItemPrice
wedding pipe organ40
wedding arch, wedding dress, wedding tuxedo20 each
bridal veil, wedding walls and flooring in each color, wedding-party wall12 each
wedding head table, wedding table, wedding shoes, wedding pumps6 each
wedding bench, wedding cake, wedding welcome board5 each
wedding candle set, wedding flower stand, wedding rugs4 each
wedding chair, wedding decoration3 each

Buying one of everything comes to about 260 crystals. Since Cyrus only sells 8 items a day, it'll take several days to buy it all even if you've saved up enough crystals, so don't leave your shopping until the end of the month.

Cyrus isn't the only source of wedding-themed items. In June, Able Sisters stocks the shiromuku, tsunokakushi, and white hakama with crest, and Nook Shopping carries the nuptial bell, nuptial doorplate, nuptial ring pillow, and flower-petal basket. If you want to stage a wedding on your own island, you can pick these up along the way.

04How to earn crystals faster

Buy cheap items first to build up your count. Your heart crystal payout depends on how much wedding furniture is in the set, so in the first few days, go for the 3–4 crystal items like chairs, candles, and flower stands. The fuller the set, the more crystals you earn each day, and the faster it snowballs.

Save the pipe organ and arch for last. At 40 and 20 crystals, buying them early eats up several days' worth of crystals and doesn't fill the room nearly as well as a bunch of small items. Once you've got all the cheap stuff, save up for these two.

Don't take long breaks. The wedding furniture only fully unlocks after 7 shoots, so if you start after June 24, you won't be able to finish 7 before the month ends. If you start early, you've got room to skip a day or two.

TipAfter your 7th shoot, the wedding furniture comes in a few more color schemes, and Reese will start specifying colors in her requests. Pick furniture in the colors she asks for to get the full crystal payout.

05Version history

Starting with 2.0, seasonal events come around automatically every year, with no need to wait for Nintendo to push an update.

If you already got the wedding fence and wedding wand DIY recipes in a previous year, Harvey and Cyrus give you 10 heart crystals instead.

The 3.0 patch notes don't mention any changes to this event, so it plays the same as before.
