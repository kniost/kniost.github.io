Cherry-blossom season: April 1 to 10, catch petals with your net

Only 10 days a year: how to catch petals, how to learn the DIY recipes, and a materials table for all 15 cherry-blossom items

Cherry-blossom season is one of the two shortest material seasons (the other is maple leaves): April 1 to 10 in the Northern Hemisphere and October 1 to 10 in the Southern Hemisphere. During these days, the hardwood trees on your island burst into pink bloom and petals drift through the air. Swing your net to catch them, and you've got cherry-blossom petals.

Cherry-blossom petals go into 15 DIY recipes: the outdoor picnic set, cherry-blossom bonsai, cherry-blossom-trees wall, cherry-blossom umbrella, and more. Crafting all of them takes 95 petals. Once the 10 days are up, you'll have to wait until next year, so try to do a lap of your island every day and stock up on petals.

01Which 10 days

All dates are in-game dates. The Southern Hemisphere runs half a year behind the north.

HemisphereStartsEnds
NorthernApril 1April 10
SouthernOctober 1October 10

An in-game day starts at 5 AM, so late on the night of the 10th, right up until 5 AM the next morning, still counts as cherry-blossom season. You get one last night to catch a few more.

Cherry-blossom season falls entirely within young spring bamboo season, so you can keep harvesting bamboo during these 10 days too. In some years, Bunny Day in the Northern Hemisphere overlaps with cherry-blossom season, and balloons you shoot down during those days will mix in Bunny Day DIY recipes.

02How to catch petals

Cherry-blossom petals drift slowly through the air. It's just like catching bugs: aim your net and swing. Each petal sells for only 200 Bells, so it's not worth selling; keep them for furniture.

Petals only stack up to 10 per pocket slot, so your pockets will fill up fast. Put them in storage as you go to free up space and keep catching.

TipCherry-blossom petals can go in storage. Stock up near the end of the season so you can keep crafting after it's over. And if you haven't learned every recipe for the season yet, no worries — you can pull them back out once you do.

03Where the DIY recipes come from

With 15 recipes to learn in 10 days, time is tight, so you'll want to use all three sources.

The big one is shooting down balloons. During cherry-blossom season, balloons floating overhead drop this season's DIY recipes, and they'll keep doing so until you've learned them all. For these 10 days, chase down every balloon you see.

Next is visiting Resident Services on the first day. On April 1 in the north or October 1 in the south, Tom Nook or Isabelle will give you the outdoor picnic set. You only get it on the first day, so don't forget to stop by.

The third is Kapp'n's boat tours, covered below.

Missed it? Catch up on Kapp'n's boat

Kapp'n at the pier will take you out to sea once a day for 1,000 Nook Miles. Every so often, he'll drop you on an island stuck in a particular season, and the island in full cherry bloom is one of them; each seasonal island has about a 3% chance. On that island you can catch cherry-blossom petals, and message bottles on the beach may hold cherry-blossom DIY recipes. Since cherry-blossom season only lasts 10 days, this is a really useful way to fill in missing recipes.

The odds are low, so this is really just luck. There's also a catch: your own island has to have gone through this season at least once before you can be taken to the matching island. Once you've decorated your hotel's first VIP room, Kapp'n will take you on an extra trip each day (for 100 hotel tickets), which gives you a slightly better shot.

05What to make first

Outdoor picnic set: the one you get on the first day. It's a picnic blanket laid out with boxed lunches, and it takes just 10 petals. Set it under a cherry tree and you've got an instant blossom-viewing party.

Cherry-blossom-trees wall, sakura-wood flooring, cherry-blossom flooring: if you want a cherry-blossom-themed room, the walls and floors are what catch the eye. The cherry-blossom-trees wall and cherry-blossom flooring take 10 petals each, the most of anything this season.

Cherry-blossom umbrella, cherry-blossom pochette: small things to carry or wear. Just 6 or 7 petals each, and great for spring photos.

Cherry-blossom wand: this season's wand. Wave it to instantly change into an outfit you've saved.

The blossom-viewing lantern and cherry-blossom clock can also be customized in different colors once you've made them.

TipIf you don't have all 95 petals, start with the recipes you've already learned and want most, and put the rest of your petals in storage. When you learn other recipes next year, the petals you saved this year will still work.
